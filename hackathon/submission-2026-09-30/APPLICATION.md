## If no, what major features or new significant mobile development have you done?

Degen Detox is a new crypto-focused Android wellbeing experience built on disclosed foundations from my earlier Cortisol Zero app. The CLOCK IN organizers confirmed its eligibility in writing after I explained this reuse.

Morning Shield protects the start of the day from social feeds, trading charts and alarming news: users choose a 1-4-hour app-blocking window after their wake-up time, creating room for breakfast, breathing and a positive start before checking markets.

New development includes Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter purchases with SOL and SKR; exact finalized transaction checks, separate token-based entitlements and wallet-proof purchase restoration; wallet-return lifecycle fixes; native blocking/permission UX improvements, searchable app selection and persistent block notices; local Impulse Check history and time-of-day insights; a customizable evening ritual; and SKR-exclusive Touch Grass reminders with birdsong and a nature scene.

The app supports six languages and light/dark appearance. The demo was recorded on my Solana Seeker. Free breathing, education and self-observation sit alongside one-time lifetime Pro access, with no monthly subscription.

The README distinguishes inherited code/content, third-party libraries and new development. I am a self-funded solo builder using AI-assisted development and hands-on device testing. This is digital wellbeing, not addiction treatment or a clinically proven cortisol intervention.

## Does your application have an SKR integration? If so, how?

Yes. A one-time 500 SKR mainnet payment unlocks lifetime Pro plus Touch Grass, a mobile screen-break reminder configurable every 1-8 hours, with a short birdsong alert and nature-inspired scene. SOL buyers receive base Pro and can later add the SKR option for the full 500 SKR.

Purchases use Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter and an SPL transferChecked instruction. The client verifies finalized transaction evidence, including payer, recipient, exact amount, SKR mint/program/decimals and purchase memo, before unlocking the SKR entitlement. A SOL receipt cannot unlock it. Purchase restoration proves wallet ownership and checks the on-chain receipt.

SKR purchases a specific consumer utility, not staking, rewards or price exposure. Personal wellbeing notes remain on-device, not on-chain. Fees and any required token-account creation costs are disclosed separately. An unlocked QA APK lets judges review features without spending funds; the separate Mainnet APK retains real checkout.
