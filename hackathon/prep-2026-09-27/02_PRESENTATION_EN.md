# Degen Detox

## Less market noise. More of your life.

A mobile-first digital-wellbeing app for the Solana community. Built and iterated on a real Seeker, with lifetime access through SOL or SKR.

## The problem we are choosing to solve

A planned market check can turn into another refresh, another chart and another notification. The founder recognizes that pattern from personal experience and wants a calmer way to use a crypto phone.

- **Intended user:** a crypto-native person who wants boundaries, not another trading dashboard.
- **Product goal:** help the user choose a routine before an impulse arrives.
- **Positioning:** digital wellbeing, not medical treatment or financial advice.

## One daily routine

- **Protect the morning:** selected-app blocking for 1–4 hours after the chosen wake time.
- **Notice the urge:** a quick score and optional note, followed by an honest local summary.
- **Close the session:** a three-step wind-down with an optional personal fourth step.
- **Step outside:** SKR-enabled Touch Grass reminders with a short birdsong alert.

Breathing exercises and educational content are free. Recipes and additional routines complement the core loop rather than replace it.

## Native where it matters

Morning Shield uses Android Accessibility events, not a web overlay pretending to block apps. The user chooses apps and hours before committing; once active, the app does not provide an early-stop button.

Phone/system controls and supported wallets stay accessible. Permission explanations, selected-app search, named selections and remaining-time notices make the commitment understandable. Notifications respect Android sound and Do Not Disturb choices; there is no forced screen unlock.

## Solana is the purchase rail

- **Mobile Wallet Adapter:** authorize and sign in a compatible wallet; no private keys entered into Degen Detox.
- **SOL:** 0.1 SOL for lifetime Pro.
- **SKR:** 500 SKR for lifetime Pro plus Touch Grass.
- **Recovery:** wallet proof and finalized transaction verification, with distinct SOL/SKR rights.
- **No monthly subscription:** one app payment, with network/token-account costs disclosed separately.

Personal check-in notes remain on the device. Payments are public blockchain activity, not a private payment system. Verification is client-side RPC validation, not a proprietary on-chain protocol.

## SKR with a reason to leave the screen

The SKR purchase unlocks configurable 1–8-hour outdoor-break reminders, a 3.2-second birdsong notification and a nature scene. Existing SOL customers can add this later with a separate full 500 SKR payment.

No reward farming. No staking lock. No promise that a token will rise. The utility is a daily pause, bought once.

## What works today

- **Android v0.10:** Mainnet release candidate and a separate explicitly labelled review build.
- **Device feedback:** the founder reported successful real payments, app blocking and birdsong reminders on Seeker.
- **Engineering checks:** 144 Flutter tests and 22 Android logic tests.
- **Six languages:** English, Lithuanian, Spanish, French, German and Korean.

These are not retention, clinical or independent market-validation results. Next validation: independent Seeker testers, repeat routine use, friction in permission setup and notification reliability across settings.
