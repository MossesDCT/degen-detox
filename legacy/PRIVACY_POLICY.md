# Privacy Policy

**Cortisol Zero**
Last updated: March 7, 2026

## Introduction

Cortisol Zero ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Cortisol Zero (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you disagree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please discontinue use of the App.

---

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly

**Mood Journal Entries**
- Daily mood ratings (1–5 scale)
- Optional text notes you write
- Optional tags you add to entries
- Dates and timestamps

**Sleep Tracking Data**
- Sleep quality ratings (1–5 stars)
- Sleep duration (hours)
- Bedtime and wake times you enter
- Optional notes

**App Settings**
- Preferred language
- Theme preference (light/dark)
- Notification preferences
- Bedtime reminder time

**Purchase Information**
- PRO upgrade status (stored locally)
- Purchase receipt verification data (processed by Google Play Billing)

### Information Collected Automatically

**Usage Data**
- App session timestamps (stored locally)
- Daily streak counter (stored locally)
- Selected soundscapes (stored locally)

**Device Data** (only for technical support purposes)
- Device type and operating system version
- App version

---

## How We Use Your Information

All data is stored **locally on your device**. We do not:
- Upload your mood journal entries to any server
- Track your usage behavior on external servers
- Sell your personal data to third parties
- Use your data for advertising

We use your data only to:
1. Display your mood history, journal entries, and sleep data in the App
2. Calculate mood averages and trends for the AI Mood Insights feature (computed locally on your device)
3. Send bedtime reminders via local device notifications (if enabled)
4. Process in-app purchases through Google Play Billing

---

## In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time PRO upgrade processed by Google Play Billing. When you make a purchase:
- Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play
- We receive confirmation of the purchase status (purchased or not)
- We do **not** receive your payment card or banking information
- Purchase tokens are stored securely on your device

Google's privacy policy applies to payment processing: https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## Data Storage

**Local Storage Only**: All your personal data (journal entries, sleep logs, settings) is stored exclusively on your device using:
- `shared_preferences` for simple key-value data
- `SQLite` database for journal and sleep entries

Your data is **not backed up** to our servers. If you uninstall the App or clear app data, your journal entries and sleep logs will be permanently deleted.

**Recommendation**: Use your device's built-in backup system to preserve your data if you switch devices.

---

## Data Retention

Data is retained on your device until:
- You manually delete individual journal entries
- You uninstall the App
- You clear the App's data through device settings

|-----------|---------|

| `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` | Scheduled notifications |

All permissions are requested only when you enable the relevant feature. You may deny any permission without affecting core app functionality.
